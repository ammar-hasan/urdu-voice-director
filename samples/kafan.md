# Sample scene — کفن — the shroud

Sample voices: گھیسو=Deep South Asian Baritone, مادھو=Haseeb

## Source

Excerpt from Premchand's short story «کفن» (1936, public domain); text verified against https://www.rekhta.org/stories/kafan-premchand-stories?lang=ur — canonical, direction only.

```text
گھیسو: معلوم ہوتا ہے بچے گی نہیں۔ سارا دن تڑپتے ہو گیا، جا دیکھ تو آ۔
مادھو: مرنا ہی ہے تو جلدی مر کیوں نہیں جاتی؟ دیکھ کر کیا آؤں۔
گھیسو: تو بڑا بیدرد ہے بے! سال بھر جس کے ساتھ جندگانی کا سکھ بھوگا، اسی کے ساتھ اتنی بے وفائی۔
مادھو: تو مجھ سے اس کا تڑپنا اور ہاتھ پاؤں پٹکنا نہیں دیکھا جاتا۔
گھیسو: جا کر دیکھ تو، کیا حالت ہے اس کی... یہاں تو اوجھا بھی ایک روپیہ مانگتا ہے۔
مادھو: مجھے وہاں ڈر لگتا ہے۔ میری عورت جب مری تھی، تو میں تین دن تک اس کے پاس سے ہلا بھی نہیں۔ وہ مجھے دیکھ لے گی تو کھل کر ہاتھ پاؤں بھی نہ پٹک سکے گی۔
```

## Scene card

- **Source:** Premchand, «کفن» (1936), public domain — canonical published text. All six turns, both speakers, turn order, and every word protected. No refinement permitted; treatment is direction only.
- **Setting:** a winter night, rural north India. گھیسو (father, ~60) and مادھو (his son) sit by the fire; inside the hut مادھو's wife is dying in childbirth. Her agony is present only through the men's words (`تڑپنا`، `ہاتھ پاؤں پٹکنا`).
- **Relationship and register:** father to son, coarse peasant directness — `تُو` from the father (`تو بڑا بیدرد ہے بے`, `جا دیکھ تو آ`), no honorifics either way. This register is character-defining and must not be softened.
- **Anchors (source-supported):** the writhing woman named in the dialogue; `اوجھا بھی ایک روپیہ مانگتا ہے` — money pressure under the whole exchange.
- **What changes during the scene:** resigned fatalism (گھیسو) meets open callousness (مادھو) → rebuke → self-defense → insistence that collapses into the one-rupee reality → مادھو's admission of fear, his real reason, surfaced only at the end.
- **Understood but unspoken:** both men have already decided she will not survive; the horror is normalized, routine. This must play as flatness and low volume, never melodrama.
- **Emotional peak:** turn 6 — fear admitted, memory cited, rationale completed quietly. Carried by pacing and lowered voice, not by added intensity.
- **Output target:** ElevenLabs `eleven_v3` sample; caption-clean artifact required alongside.

## Turn audit

1. **گھیسو** — direction only. Beat map: `resigned conclusion already reached → flat statement → turn to son with a practical push`. Two beats: `معلوم ہوتا ہے بچے گی نہیں` is weary and settled, not alarmed; `جا دیکھ تو آ` is a low imperative, paternal habit rather than urgency. Risk to prevent: reading the opener as panic.
2. **مادھو** — direction only. Beat map: `irritation at being asked → callous rhetorical question → dismissive closure`. The cruelty lives in flatness; no anger, no heat. Risk to prevent: an outraged or dramatic reading, which would falsify the character.
3. **گھیسو** — direction only. Beat map: `the son's answer lands → contained rebuke → grave moral appeal`. Beat 1 (`تو بڑا بیدرد ہے بے!`) is scolding under control — an old man's rebuke, not shouting. Beat 2 (`سال بھر جس کے ساتھ...`) slows and gains weight; it is the scene's one moral sentence and must not be rushed.
4. **مادھو** — direction only. Beat map: `rebuke received → excuse offered as explanation → quick, unbroken delivery`. He answers at once; the justification is rehearsed. No guilt audible — the point is that there is none.
5. **گھیسو** — direction only. Beat map: `one more push → the source's own ellipsis marks the turn → insistence gives way to the money reality`. The `...` is canonical and functional: reluctance before naming cost. Beat 2 lands flat; poverty is stated as fact, not lamented.
6. **مادھو** — direction only. Beat map: `money named → the real obstacle surfaces → fear admitted hesitantly → memory slows the pace → rationale completed quietly`. Three beats. The peak: `مجھے وہاں ڈر لگتا ہے` must cost him something; the memory (`تین دن تک... ہلا بھی نہیں`) decelerates; the final clause (`کھل کر ہاتھ پاؤں بھی نہ پٹک سکے گی`) is delivered as settled, almost reasonable logic — which is what makes it chilling.
