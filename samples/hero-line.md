```text
خالد: سچ یہی ہے کہ میں ہر وہ صبح یاد کرتا ہوں جس میں تم چائے کے دو کپ رکھتی تھیں... اور میں پھر بھی دیر سے لوٹا۔
```

## Decision

- Mode: fictional dialogue.
- Protected: every fact and turn; familiar `تم`; the possibility that things cannot return to how they were; خالد’s sole responsibility; رخسانہ’s years of loneliness and nightly waiting; the condition about another lie; and the remembered mornings, two cups of tea, and late returns.
- No refinement: the source already speaks in credible thought units. Its echoes, ellipses, contrasts, and repeated limiting constructions (`بس`, `تو`, `اب بھی`, `پھر بھی`) do useful work.
- Direction follows the stated argument only: limiting a claim, accepting responsibility, challenging the adequacy of that statement, denying any power to erase the past, asking what remains, requiring a truthful answer, and giving the remembered example. It does not add reconciliation, forgiveness, regret, or another motive.
