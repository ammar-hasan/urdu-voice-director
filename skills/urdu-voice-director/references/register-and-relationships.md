# Register and relationships

Register is a system, not a synonym swap. Pronouns, verb agreement, address terms, requests, particles, and directness must move together.

## Address scale

| Form | Typical force | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| `آپ` | respect, distance, care, formality, or household norm | Can sound cold if introduced into an intimate scene without cause. |
| `تم` | familiarity, peer relation, ordinary intimacy | Can be disrespectful where `آپ` is established. |
| `تُو` | extreme intimacy, poetic/devotional usage, scolding, contempt, or specific regional/family norm | High-risk; never introduce casually. |

These are context-sensitive tendencies. Urdu encodes respect through pronouns and matching verb morphology, but region, generation, class, household, and dramatic context change the social meaning.

## Agreement check

Keep the full cluster consistent:

```text
آپ کہاں جا رہے ہیں؟
تم کہاں جا رہے ہو؟
```

Do not change only the pronoun and leave mismatched possessives or verb endings.

## Relationship cues

- **Close friends:** may use names, `یار`, `بھئی`, teasing, ellipsis, direct questions, and `تم`; none is mandatory.
- **Siblings:** often fast and elliptical; age hierarchy may affect commands and address.
- **Parent–child:** may combine authority, affection, names, kinship terms, and household-specific respect.
- **Teacher–student:** usually clearer turn structure and institutional respect; avoid caricatured formality.
- **Strangers/service encounters:** use respectful requests and `آپ` unless the scene establishes otherwise.
- **Elders:** do not make them archaic by default. Age affects experience and pace, not automatic literary vocabulary.

## Formal respect without stiffness

Stiff:

```text
کیا آپ مجھے اس امر سے آگاہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ اجلاس کب شروع ہوگا؟
```

Natural formal:

```text
کیا آپ بتا سکتے ہیں کہ اجلاس کب شروع ہوگا؟
```

## Register shifts are story events

A sudden `آپ` after established `تم`, a name replacing `امی`, or omission of `جی` may signal anger, distance, public formality, irony, or growth. Preserve such shifts unless they are clearly accidental. If you add one, explain why.
